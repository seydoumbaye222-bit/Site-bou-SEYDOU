European scholarships for Senegalese students: new doors opened by the Africa‑EU partnership 2024

← Retour à l'accueil

The new Africa‑EU cooperation framework signed in 2024 opens a series of scholarship programmes targeting Senegalese students wishing to pursue their studies at European universities. This initiative aims to strengthen academic mobility, encourage skill sharing, and support the development of human capital in Senegal.

An unprecedented cooperation framework between Africa and the European Union

The Africa‑EU 2024 partnership is based on a bilateral agreement signed in Brussels, which provides for the creation of funds dedicated to student mobility, the simplification of visa procedures, and mutual recognition of degrees. For Senegal, this is the first time that several European countries have jointly committed to financing higher education for its citizens.

The most sought‑after scholarships for Senegalese

Among the flagship programmes are the Erasmus+ Master scholarship, the Marie‑Skłodowska‑Curie research scholarship, and the national scholarships offered by the ministries of Education of France, Germany, and the Netherlands. They cover tuition fees, accommodation, health insurance and, often, a monthly stipend.

How to apply: key steps and advice

Applications generally open between February and June. First, identify the programme, prepare a complete dossier (transcripts, motivation letter, letters of recommendation) and submit the request via Senegal’s national portal or directly on the host university’s website. Support is provided by the guidance offices of Senegalese universities.

Expected impact on Senegalese student mobility

Early feedback indicates a 30 % increase in the number of Senegalese students enrolled in Europe, as well as better professional integration upon their return. The scholarships are also seen as a lever to strengthen research collaborations between African and European institutions.

Conclusion

Through the Africa‑EU 2024 partnership, Senegalese students now have an expanded range of European scholarships, enabling them to access high‑level training while contributing to Senegal’s visibility on the international stage.
