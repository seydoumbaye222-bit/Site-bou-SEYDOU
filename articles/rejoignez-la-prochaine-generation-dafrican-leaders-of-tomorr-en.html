The 2024 cohort of scholars in the African Leaders of Tomorrow (ALOT) program has just released its first feedback, illustrating how intensive training and funding have turned their projects into levers of social and economic impact in Africa.

Missions assigned to ALOT 2024 scholars

The awardees are invited to lead concrete initiatives in their fields (entrepreneurship, health, energy, education), to develop viable business plans, to mobilize local stakeholders, and to measure impact indicators during and after the program.

Desired profile: key skills and values

The selector seeks dynamic young leaders capable of systemic thinking, with field experience, a sharp sense of ethics, proficiency in French or English, and a strong motivation to contribute to the sustainable development of their community.

Inspiring journeys and impact projects

Among the 12 scholars are Aïssa (Senegal), who launched a digital agriculture platform that reduces waste by 30%, Fatou (Côte d’Ivoire), who created a mobile school for rural areas, and Kwame (Ghana), who is developing a micro‑solar grid powering 5,000 households.

Practical advice for applying to the next cohort

1) Write a motivation letter focused on your impact project; 2) Attach a 10‑slide pitch deck detailing the problem, the solution, and the business model; 3) Obtain two recommendation letters from mentors or employers; 4) Prepare for a video interview centered on your values and your five‑year vision.

Video and written testimonials: what the scholars say

The videos posted on the official website feature scholars sharing their challenges, successes, and future expectations; the blog articles highlight the value of the ALOT network, personalized support, and the visibility offered to industry partners.

Conclusion

The testimonies of the 2024 cohort demonstrate that the African Leaders of Tomorrow program is a springboard for turning ambitious ideas into concrete, sustainable projects; future applicants are invited to draw inspiration from these journeys and start preparing their dossiers now for the next selection.
